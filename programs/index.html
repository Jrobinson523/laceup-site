Booking

How it works

  1. Book a free discovery call. Pick a time online, or DM us on Instagram.
  2. Talk goals. A Lace Up coach calls you. We ask about the player's level, goals and schedule.
  3. Get on the schedule. We match the player to 1:1, small group, team or clinic. Pricing is covered on the call.

Families can train more than once a week, up to four days, and choose what fits their schedule.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need to be at a certain athletic level to train with Lace Up?

No. Lace Up works with players from beginner through varsity/AAU. Each plan starts from where the athlete is right now.

What are your coaching credentials?

Coach Robinson (James Robinson Jr.) is a 3-time collegiate national champion as head coach with 30+ years of coaching experience. Coach Vaughn Dweck has 35+ years of coaching experience, including a 2000 NYC PSAL city championship as head coach and 8 Bronx division championships. Full lists are on the About page.

How do I book a session?

Book a free discovery call, or DM us on Instagram. We'll talk through goals and get you on the schedule.

What does training cost?

Ask on your discovery call. Sessions are set up per player, so we go over it with you directly.
