Foundation.

Our philosophy, from Coach Robinson and Coach Vaughn

James Robinson Jr.3x collegiate national champion as head coach
Vaughn Dweck2000 NYC PSAL city champion as head coach

Between us, we have coached for more than 65 years: Coach Robinson at the college level, Coach Vaughn in New York City high schools. A fifth grader on day one gets the same start from us that our championship teams got. Footwork, ball handling, shot mechanics.

Those three are the FOUNDATION of every Lace Up session. A player with good feet, a tight handle and a shot they can repeat can play for any coach, in any system, at whatever level they reach.

We push hard, we teach the details, and we get to know every player's family. Mindset gets trained the same way footwork does, on every rep.

LACE UP

Lace Up Sports is a basketball training and development program in northern Westchester. Trainers James Robinson Jr. and Vaughn Dweck bring 65+ years of combined coaching experience to players from beginner through varsity and AAU, in Cortlandt, Peekskill, Yorktown, Croton, Ossining and Mohegan Lake.
