The Lace Up way

  1. Fundamentals

    Every session starts with footwork, ball handling, and shot mechanics, no matter the player's age or level.

  2. Mindset

    Robinson and Dweck train mindset the same way they train footwork, every rep and every drill.

  3. Personalized

    Every plan starts with the player in front of us, in a one-on-one session, a small group or the full squad.

  4. Family

    Robinson and Dweck know the families they train as well as the players.

Questions

Do I need to be at a certain athletic level to train with Lace Up?

No. Lace Up works with players from beginner through varsity/AAU. Each plan starts from where the athlete is right now.

What are your coaching credentials?

James Robinson Jr. is a 3-time collegiate national champion as head coach with 30+ years of coaching experience. Vaughn Dweck has 35+ years of coaching experience, including a 2000 NYC PSAL city championship as head coach and 8 Bronx division championships. Full lists are in the About section.

How do I book a session?

Book a free discovery call, or DM us on Instagram. We'll talk through goals and get you on the schedule.

What does training cost?

Ask on your discovery call. Sessions are set up per player, so we go over it with you directly.
