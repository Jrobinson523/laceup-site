About Lace Up

Our Organization

Lace Up sports is a holistic basketball development, training, and sports navigation organization. We enable student athletes to reach their personal and athletic goals. Our nurturing environment coupled with using "growth mindset" techniques has helped many of our student athletes reach the pinnacle of the sports world and beyond. Our staff is committed to pushing each player to the next level. We present an intense and detailed approach to training and playing the game.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create young people who approach every obstacle as an opportunity. Our goal is to successfully develop each student athletes' confidence, creativity, leadership, teamwork, and sportsmanship.

Our Offer

Lace Up provides highly seasoned and respected coaches so our student athletes can compete at the highest level. Family and quality instruction are the cornerstones of Lace Up. Our coaches are dedicated to developing relationships with our student athletes and their families.

Questions, answered

Do I need to be at a certain athletic level to train with Lace Up?

No. Lace Up works with players from beginner through varsity/AAU. Each plan starts from where the athlete is right now.

What are your coaching credentials?

Coach Robinson (James Robinson Jr.) is a 3-time collegiate national champion as head coach with 30+ years of coaching experience. Coach Vaughn Dweck has 35+ years of coaching experience, including a 2000 NYC PSAL city championship as head coach and 8 Bronx division championships. Full lists are in the About section.

How do I book a session?

Book a free discovery call below, or call/text us directly. We'll talk through goals and get you on the schedule.

What does training cost?

Ask on your discovery call. Sessions are set up per player, so we go over it with you directly.
