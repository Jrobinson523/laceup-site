The Lace Up way

James Robinson Jr. won three national titles as a college head coach. Vaughn Dweck won a New York City PSAL title as a high school head coach. Every Lace Up plan is built on the same four things.

Our philosophy

  • Fundamentals

    Every session starts with footwork, ball handling and shot mechanics, no matter the player's age or level.

  • Mindset

    Robinson and Dweck train mindset the same way they train footwork, on every rep and every drill.

  • Personalized

    Every plan starts with the player in front of us, in a one-on-one session, a small group or the full squad.

  • Family

    Robinson and Dweck know the families they train as well as the players.

How we train

Every session starts with footwork, ball handling and shot mechanics. Then we build the player's confidence, creativity, leadership, teamwork and sportsmanship on top of them.

The training is intense and detailed. We train with growth-mindset techniques, so players learn to treat every obstacle as an opportunity. Families pick the schedule that works for them, up to four days a week.
